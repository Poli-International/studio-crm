Material Certification Checker Documentation

Comprehensive guide to body jewelry biocompatibility, mill certifications, and ASTM/ISO standards

Overview

The Material Certification Checker is an essential reference and validation tool for professional body piercers, studio owners, and jewelry buyers. It provides instant verification of material standards, biocompatibility specifications, and supplier documentation criteria.

With the rapid influx of unverified imports in the body art industry, verifying genuine mill certifications and understanding biocompatibility standards (ASTM F136, ASTM F138, ISO 5832, ISO 10993) is crucial for client safety and preventing adverse tissue reactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 'Implant Grade'?

This means the material has been manufactured, tested, and certified for surgical implantation inside the human body (e.g., ASTM F136 titanium, ASTM F138 steel).

Is 316L Steel safe for initial piercings?

Generic 316L is surgical-grade but not necessarily implant-grade. Implant-grade steel must conform to ASTM F138 (316LVM) vacuum melt specifications.

Can I use Sterling Silver in a new piercing?

Never. Silver oxidizes upon contact with body fluids, which can cause permanent skin discoloration (argyria) and irritation.

Is Gold safe for healing?

Only if it is 14k or 18k solid gold that is certified nickel-free. Gold-plated or vermeil jewelry must never be used in initial piercings.

What is BioFlex?

BioFlex® body jewelry is a medical-grade PP-R random copolymer that is 100% biocompatible, flexible, steam sterilizable, and highly recommended for initial healing and medical procedures.
