Studio Password

No password is set: anyone on the studio network can open the CRM.

Roles

What each role may open and change. Give a person a role in the staff list (Edit, Access level). A role without an area does not see its screen, and the CRM refuses its changes there.

Tablet Kiosk PIN

No PIN is set: anyone holding the tablet can leave Kiosk Mode.

Kiosk Tablets

A tablet that only shows the forms the desk sends it. On the tablet, open this address in the browser:

The first time, the tablet warns that the connection is not private. Tap Advanced, then Proceed (Chrome) or Continue (Edge): it is your own server, the certificate was made by it.

If the warning keeps coming back, or a kiosk browser stops on it, use this address instead. No warning, but the forms cross your network unencrypted, so only on the studio's own Wi-Fi:

Give this computer a fixed address in your router ("DHCP reservation" or "static IP"), or the tablets lose it when the address changes.

Set the studio password first: the kiosk password comes on top of it.

Studio details

Printed on every consent form, on the computer and on the kiosk tablets.

Stations

Your rooms and chairs. A station's usual artist is chosen for it when you book that artist.

Aftercare sheets

The sheets you print for clients. Change any wording, in each language; a language left empty prints the English.

Database Backup

The CRM saves a full copy of the studio database every day in the storage/backups folder on the computer that runs it, and keeps the last 30 days.

A folder on a USB stick, another disk, or a Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox folder on this computer, so a dead disk or a stolen computer does not take every copy with it. Leave it empty for none.

Updates

Checking for a new version...

Your data stays where it is and is backed up first. The new version is tried on a copy before it is installed, and the CRM restarts by itself.
