📏 Industrial & Scaffold Sizing

Industrial Barbell Length Calculator

Calculate the exact barbell shaft length needed between two cartilage punctures based on measured hole-to-hole distance, ball protrusion, and healing stage tissue clearance.

What to Measure Measure with precision calipers from the outer edge of the entrance hole across the ear to the outer edge of the exit hole. This is your hole-to-hole distance. The calculator automatically adds ball protrusion (+3mm to +5mm) and healing clearance (+4mm fresh, +2mm healed).
