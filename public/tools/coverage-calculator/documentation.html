Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate for a full sleeve?

Measure the arm as a cylinder (circumference × length) and input the total square inches for the most accurate result.

Does the tool factor in different ink brands?

The calculator uses standard viscosity averages. High-pigment load 'super-blacks' may require 10% less volume than standard inks.

Can I calculate coverage for watercolor styles?

Yes, the 'Complexity' slider allows you to reduce the saturation percentage to match low-opacity techniques.

Is the wastage factor included?

Yes, we include a standard 15% 'cup and tube' waste margin in the final pigment volume recommendation.

How precise is the area estimation?

The tool uses geometric projection. For highly irregular shapes, we recommend breaking the design into smaller primitive shapes (circles/rectangles).
