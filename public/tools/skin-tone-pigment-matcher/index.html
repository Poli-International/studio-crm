Framing & Professional Scope

This tool reflects general artist-community consensus and directional professional observations across skin phototypes. It is not a clinical or peer-reviewed measurement. Actual tattoo longevity and healed vibrancy depend on needle technique, ink formulation, saturation, placement, aftercare, individual immune response, and UV exposure.
