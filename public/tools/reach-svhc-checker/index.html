SVHC Coverage & Screening Scope

This checker monitors 51 curated Substances of Very High Concern (SVHCs) out of the 244 official entries on the ECHA Candidate List (curated snapshot as of 27 June 2024).

The curated subset focuses specifically on substances with documented presence or high risk in tattoo inks, permanent makeup, body jewelry alloys, and studio consumables:

  • Heavy metals & metal compounds (lead, cadmium, hexavalent chromium, cobalt salts, nickel)
  • Primary aromatic amines (PAAs) and azo cleavage precursors
  • Phthalate plasticizers found in synthetic polymers and packaging
  • Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) associated with Carbon Black (CI 77266)
  • Industrial solvents, preservatives, and glycol ethers
Note: Negative screening in this tool does not guarantee absence of other SVHCs or compliance with REACH Annex XVII restrictions (such as entry 75 for tattoo inks). Always obtain full supplier SDS declarations.
