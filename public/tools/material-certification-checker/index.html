Understanding Material Certifications

🏆 What Are Mill Certifications?

Mill test reports certify the chemical composition, tensile strength, and metallurgical properties of raw material lots. They provide documentary proof of compliance with ASTM or ISO standards.

📋 How to Verify Materials

Always request authentic mill certifications from manufacturers. Verified suppliers provide lot-tracked test certificates detailing chemical analysis and mechanical properties.

⚠️ Common Discrepancies

Watch for unverified marketing terms like generic "surgical steel" without ASTM F138 documentation, or "hypoallergenic" claims lacking alloy breakdown.

✅ Initial Piercing Materials

Established professional practice specifies biocompatible materials for initial piercings: ASTM F136 titanium, ASTM F138 surgical steel, BioFlex® body jewelry (PP-R), niobium, and borosilicate glass.

🔬 Biocompatibility Testing

Biocompatibility is evaluated through cytotoxicity testing (ISO 10993-5), dermal sensitisation (ISO 10993-10) and USP Class VI conformity for medical-grade polymers.

⚡ Sterilization Protocols

Initial jewelry must withstand steam autoclave sterilization (121°C to 134°C). Titanium, implant steel, niobium, BioFlex® body jewelry, and borosilicate glass are autoclave-compatible.
