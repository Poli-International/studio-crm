☀️ HIGH UV EXPOSURE ZONE

High UV exposure area selected. Prioritize photo-protection during healing.

  • Zero direct sun during active healing (Weeks 1-4). Broken epidermal barrier cannot produce protective melanin evenly and risks hyperpigmentation or scar darkening.
  • Never apply chemical lotions or sunscreens directly onto open puncture wounds or peeling tattoo scabs. Use physical shade, wide-brim hats, or UPF 50+ clothing.
  • Once fully re-epithelialized, apply broad-spectrum mineral sunscreen (Zinc Oxide or Titanium Dioxide SPF 30+) daily to preserve tattoo pigment and prevent piercing scar tissue darkening.

    3 Comprehensive Tattoo Longevity Care Plan

    Essential protocols to protect pigment vibrancy and prevent premature ink dispersion.

    🧴

    Daily Protocol

    • Hydrate with fragrance-free, water-based moisturizer.
    • Apply broad-spectrum SPF 50+ to exposed tattoo skin 15 min prior to going outside.
    • Avoid vigorous scrubbing with abrasive loofahs over tattooed skin.
    📅

    Weekly Routine

    • Gentle exfoliation around (not aggressively over) healed tattoo borders to remove dead skin cells.
    • Deep hydration treatment with jojoba oil or ceramide balm.
    • Check for any localized dryness or skin irritation.
    ☀️

    Sun & Outdoor Protocol

    • Reapply SPF 50+ every 2 hours during continuous outdoor activity.
    • Utilize UPF 50+ UV-protective clothing for beach or hiking days.
    • Rinse promptly after swimming in chlorinated pools or saltwater.
