Measure Your Own Piece (Reverse Lookup)

Measure your post or ring thickness using digital calipers, then enter the value to find the closest gauge and variance.

How to Measure with Calipers

  • 1. Zero your calipers while fully closed.
  • 2. Measure the thickness of the post or wire directly across the shaft, avoiding balls, flares, or threads.
  • 3. Take three gentle readings along the wearable area to confirm uniformity.
